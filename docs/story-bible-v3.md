# Jacob: Neon Pilgrimage — Production Story Bible v3
Status: WORKING PRODUCTION BASELINE. Do not mistake proposed scenes for original campaign canon.

## Core premise
Jacob, an ordinary apprentice craftsperson, travels to the giant king's lands alongside the Titans League adventuring team, the Super Naturals. He learns from people and builds friendships while protected by heroes. The League teleports the adventurers away after its obligations conclude, leaving Jacob and other non-adventurers stranded. Jacob attempts to get home, gathering a comparatively vulnerable caravan. Every earlier relationship may offer later choices. There is no required perfect route, only a homecoming with different costs.

## Established canon
- Titans League is a professional sporting entertainment league; giants watch smaller races as spectacles.
- Giants originally brought races to the region for entertainment; giants see themselves as superior.
- Chest carried to the giant king is League payment/tribute, not necessarily a secret doomsday device.
- Super Naturals: Apex, Dedor, Theramous, Widjet; existing D&D player characters. Their detailed histories await original-player confirmation.
- League teleports the adventuring party away; Jacob is left behind, not betrayed as part of a secret conspiracy.
- The first half accumulates stories, skills, relationships, possessions; the second half tests their use.
- NPC stories should be compelling independently of their mechanical rewards.
- No mandatory 'correct NPC' or single required survival solution.
- Homecoming asks 'At what cost?' and 'Was it worth it?' without prescribing a winning ending.

## Four-act spine
1. THE BORROWED ADVENTURE — Neon, workshop, League acquaintances, Super Naturals, journey, trials under their protection.
2. THE PRICE OF WONDER — giant city, ceremony/tribute, personal reflection; League teleports heroes away, leaving non-adventurers stranded.
3. THE LONG ROAD HOME — Jacob gathers Brann, Mira, Kest and potentially Orlan, Pip, Lysa and children; survival obstacles echo outward-journey learning; ambush may kill a guide, but death is not inevitable.
4. THE WEIGHT OF HOME — survival climax belongs primarily to Act III; Neon return, master notices changed craft, companions part, gift/keepsake echoes opening, individualized reckoning.

## Design invariants
- Every challenge has a baseline route plus several earned alternatives.
- Permanent knowledge and privileges remain available; favors and supplies may be spent; relationships can change.
- Never require one particular bond, class, resource or prior choice to finish.
- Do not invent details of original player-character backstories; mark UNKNOWN and expand later.
- Art references use stable IDs, not embedded prose filenames.
- Old data/panels.json remains untouched until runtime migration is tested.
